The Symplecticity Proof: Why Energy Doesn't BleedThe core problem with standard numerical differential equation solvers (like standard Runge-Kutta 4) is that they introduce artificial numerical friction or artificial energy inflation. The specification proves that the Velocity Verlet algorithm avoids this by factoring the integration step into three sequential shear maps in phase space: Half-Step Momentum Kick: Adjusts momentum \[\mathbf{p}\] based on the force gradient.Full-Step Coordinate Drift: Moves position \[\mathbf{q}\] linearly using the new momentum.Second Half-Step Momentum Kick: Readjusts momentum \[\mathbf{p}\] at the final position. 💡 The Alternating Property The geometric proof hinges on taking the exterior derivative of these shifts.
